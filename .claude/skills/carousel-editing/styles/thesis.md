# Thesis

**Use for:** coined concepts ("the beige-ification of founder brands"), dichotomies, contrarian claims, belief shifts. These are the hook patterns from the Hook Pattern Library that outperformed for the reference creator, and they need no photo.

- One statement per slide, Inter Display SemiBold, left-aligned, block optically centered. Optional small caps `kicker` (the series name, e.g. "Boring business lessons") and a short `body` paragraph (≤ 25 words).
- Themes: `light` (off-white #F4F1EC, ink text, highlighter-swipe accent) and `dark` (#111111, off-white text, butter-yellow accent). Alternate them to give the swipe rhythm: dark cover, light body, dark payoff, light CTA.
- Accent: exactly one `*word*` per slide, on the word the slide is about.
- Render: `"style": "thesis"`, `lines`, `kicker`, `body`, `theme`.
