# Carousel styles

Three named styles. `../SKILL.md` step 0 explains how Claude picks one. All three share the **signature accent**: one `*word*` per slide in Instrument Serif Italic, butter yellow #F5D547 (on light backgrounds, ink-colored italic on a butter-yellow highlighter swipe). Talking heads and voiceover montages use the same accent, so every format reads as the user's.

| Style | Looks like | Needs | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Photo Stack](photo-stack.md) | Full-bleed real photo, big cream type stacked low-left | 4+ strong vertical photos (≥ 1000 px, calm lower half) | Stories, money and access numbers, taste reveals |
| [Thesis](thesis.md) | One big statement per slide on off-white or near-black | Nothing but words | Coined concepts, dichotomies, contrarian claims, belief shifts |
| [Notes](notes.md) | Off-white paper, a typed title, a handwritten numbered list, one item circled | Optional: the user's own handwriting PNGs | Frameworks, steps, lists (Skateboard → car, Freedom math, Open the door) |

**Mixing rule:** one style per carousel, except the cover. A strong photo can always be the Photo Stack cover of a Thesis or Notes carousel. And a Thesis carousel can carry one Notes slide when a single slide is a list (it's the same paper, ink and accent).

Carousels are their own format. Don't borrow talking-head devices (captions, scrolling quotes, callouts) or montage devices.
