# Notes

**Use for:** frameworks, steps and lists. It's the carousel cousin of the talking-head Explainer Board (same paper, ink and handwriting), but static.

- Typed title in Inter Display SemiBold with the accent word, then a handwritten numbered list (2–5 items, one size for all), with one item circled (`circle`: its index).
- Handwriting: the user's own drawings first (a `drawing` PNG from the post's Drive `drawings/` folder; a white background is keyed out automatically). The Caveat Bold fallback is for list text. Say in the delivery note when the fallback was used.
- Background: `light` (#F4F1EC, default) or `white`.
- Render: `"style": "notes"`, `title`, `items`, `circle`, `drawing`, `hand_size`.
